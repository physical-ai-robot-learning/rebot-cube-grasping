## 2. Key levers (why the config is the way it is — all settled by data)
1. **grasp_tilt 6°** (≈vertical). In the sweep, 60° (=approach 120° from top-normal) collapsed to 8% — a parallel jaw cannot wrap a table cube at 30°-above-horizontal. Measured forward offset is 0mm (demos centered) → the "forward grasping" seen early on was policy immaturity.
2. **reward rebalance** (escape from a shaping local optimum): the problem where the policy only hovered and avoided grasping. `success_bonus 80→300, fall_penalty 5→1, w_grasp 0.5→3, hover terms (reach/xy_align/vertical/wrist_center/hold) ×0.2-0.5, keep w_lift=25 with 1.5M anneal` (shaping_coef also multiplies r_lift, so do not accelerate the anneal).
3. **z_hover 0.23→0.15**: a high cam-down hover could not reach the workspace extremes (rear y0.53 dead zone, corners) → at 0.15, back 29→95%, demo SR 65→80%, no_grasp 238→34.
4. **LOOK-AT reach** (`reach_look_at`, user's idea): **aim** the wrist cam at the cube (J4/J5 pitch) to put the cube at the wrist image center (vis 0→1). The cause was straight-down aim + cam mount parallax pushing the cube above the frame (vn≈-1.6). → wrist (u,v) and yaw become usable.
5. **analytic image-yaw token**: `_a_wrist` projects the cube center + a face-direction point into the wrist and computes (cos4θ,sin4θ) (obs_dim 41). Exact (no CNN needed) → verifies the **learning** of the policy aligning the jaw to rotated faces via J6 roll. (CNN is the deploy sensor, item 5 below.)

+ Bug fix: gate where the wrist CNN was masked by the analytic shortcut when `obs_analytic_uv` and never used (pick_env).
